1. The data files (Data/*.lua)
   Generated by _build/build.py from the data files of the ForeverDB addon
   (https://foreverdb.net/addon): its stat weights, gear pools, item stats,
   item sources and enchant lists. The best-in-slot ladders are ForeverDB's:
   the generator ranks ForeverDB's gear pools with ForeverDB's own scoring.
   Unlike the foreverdb.net/bis pages, the PvE lists keep the PvP-rank
   rewards, ranked on their stats like any other item; the addon leaves the
   PvP rewards out of its PvE lists until its option "PvP rewards in the PvE
   list" is switched on, and counts them in its PvP lists until the same
   option is switched off there.
   Two things are changed from ForeverDB's data, from what the game itself
   shows (read with the developer's tool in _build/capture_addon); the first
   lines of Data/Lists.lua say how many of each:
     - caster weapons are ranked with the healing, spell damage and damage
       per second the game shows, which ForeverDB's data lacks;
     - items the game does not have are left out of the lists, and pieces it
       has that ForeverDB does not list are added, as items with no known
       source.
   One thing is added to ForeverDB's data: where an item or an enchant
   comes from, for the ones its data gives no source, read in QuestieDB
   (section 4). Those sources carry the label t in Data/Items.lua and
   Data/Enchants.lua, and the addon marks them as not confirmed in Forever.
   The pieces sold against Theramore Expeditionary Force, which ForeverDB's
   data leaves to both sides, are given to the Alliance: its vendor and every
   quest of the faction are the Alliance's alone (section 4).
   Those changes are Easy BIS's, not ForeverDB's (_build/corrections.py).
   Copyright (C) 2026 ForeverDB.net. GPL-3.0 with an attribution term
   (GPL-3.0 section 7(b)). If you reuse these files or data taken from them:
     - keep the line that starts
         -- Data: ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net)
       at the top of every data file, and
     - show this credit wherever your work lists its credits and sources:
         Data from ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net)
   ForeverDB's own LICENSE.txt and NOTICE.txt are kept next to the generator's
   copy of its data, in _build/cache/ForeverDB.

2. The generator (_build/*.py)
   Its scoring and ranking re-implement those of the ForeverDB addon
   (GearFinder.lua, Auto.lua), Copyright (C) 2026 ForeverDB.net, GPL-3.0 with
   the same attribution term.

3. Classic data
   Item sources labelled "Classic data" in ForeverDB's data are derived from
   CMaNGOS classic-db (https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db), the open World
   of Warcraft Classic 1.12 world database, Copyright (C) the CMaNGOS project
   and its contributors, GPL-3.0.

4. QuestieDB
   The sources labelled t (the quest, the creature, the vendor, the chest or
   the container an item comes from, and the names of the Classic raids) are
   read in QuestieDB (https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB), the database of
   the Questie addon, by _build/questie.py, and kept in
   _build/corrections/sources.json. Copyright (C) the Questie team and its
   contributors, GPL-3.0 as its LICENSE.md says, with the reservation it
   makes for contributions older than that licence. Nothing of QuestieDB's
   code is part of Easy BIS, and Easy BIS does not need Questie to run.
   Data/Reputation.lua says which reputation an item or an enchant asks for,
   as ForeverDB's Items.lua and Recipes.lua give it, and what QuestieDB holds
   of the quests that ask for a reputation or earn it, their vendors and
   where those stand. It is read by _build/reputations.py and kept in
   _build/corrections/reputations.json.

5. Drops counted in Forever
   The sources labelled w (the boss that drops an item and how often) come
   from the loot counts recorded by Wowhead Forever
   (https://www.wowhead.com/forever), as two addons shared by players hold
   them: DungeonJournal by Exehn and GearQuest Forever by Henrik8210. They
   are read by _build/drops.py and kept in _build/corrections/drops.json.
   Nothing of those addons' code is part of Easy BIS, and Easy BIS does not
   need them to run. Neither addon states a licence: their authors can ask
   for this data to be removed, and it will be.
